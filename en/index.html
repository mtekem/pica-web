What is PICA

PICA in seven minutes.

The starting point

Before you decide on a number, three questions.

A plant room produces thousands of readings a day. They support a decision only when each one can show where it comes from and what it is worth.

When was it measured?

Every reading keeps its measurement time and its age. A device that is still online cannot hide a variable that has stopped being measured.

Where does it come from?

Every signal keeps the equipment, the measuring point and the reading frequency it comes from.

Has anyone changed it?

Every record carries a digital fingerprint that reveals any later alteration.

How it works

Five steps, in this order.

Each step uses only what the previous one has verified.

  1. 01

    Measure

    Reads the equipment as it is installed.

  2. 02

    Validate

    Checks that each reading is fit for decisions and flags the ones that are not.

  3. 03

    Understand

    Relates the data to the engineering of the equipment and explains why things happen.

  4. 04

    Prove

    Builds a record that a third party can recalculate.

  5. 05

    Ask

    An assistant that answers only about what has been measured and validated at your site.

When a reading is missing, PICA says so and names the cause. Every figure cites its series, its period and its calculation.

After an adjustment

Did consumption fall because the plant ran better, or because demand was lower?

Comparing the month before with the month after mixes the adjustment with the weather and the demand. PICA compares observed consumption with a baseline adjusted to the same conditions. The difference that remains is the one that can be attributed to the adjustment, and a third party can recalculate it.

  • Energy in: fuel or electricity.
  • Service the plant delivers: hot water or space heating, for example.
  • Outdoor temperature for the same period.

What you get

Your facility, measured and explained.

Information ready to operate, maintain and decide.

    As installed, at the frequency each signal needs.
